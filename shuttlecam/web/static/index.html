Camera spot

    How to film
    1. Phone on a stand behind one baseline, sideways (landscape), with the court lines and both net posts in view.
    2. Don't move it between clips. If you do, pick "New camera spot".
    3. Film at 60 fps or faster. 1080p is plenty.
    4. Trim each clip to start about a second before the smash, with the shuttle coming in, and end after it lands.
    5. Upload the original from your camera roll, not a copy sent through a chat app: recompression can wipe out the shuttle.

    Mark the court

    Once per camera spot. Tap each point on the picture, then drag to fine-tune it. Skip any you can't see. You need at least 6, and a net post top helps a lot.
